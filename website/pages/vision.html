Vision and mission

Why we're building Gink, and what we promise to keep.

Our vision

A world where artificial intelligence feels personal, private and empowering, not distant or controlled by a few companies. Everyone should have their own intelligent partner that learns, remembers and grows alongside them.

The future of AI isn't about replacing people. It's about amplifying them.

True intelligence starts with understanding the individual, not just answering questions. That's why Gink isn't just another assistant: it's a step toward a more human kind of technology.

Our mission

To build an assistant that truly listens, learns and acts, blending voice, memory and action into one simple experience.

  • Understand your world through voice, context and personalisation.
  • Work across your devices, uniting your digital life in one place.
  • Respect your privacy, with you in control of what's stored, synced and remembered.
  • Empower everyone, whatever your language, device, country or way of paying.

Core goals

  1. Redefine the assistant: an AI that acts, not just responds.
  2. Privacy by design: on-device wake word, no keys in the app, your data deletable at any time.
  3. Grow with you: long-term memory, instructions and a briefing that knows what you were doing.
  4. Empower creation: help people write, code and create faster.
  5. Build an open ecosystem: open development on GitHub, with plugins and community contributions to come.

Our promise

  • Personal: built around you.
  • Transparent: no hidden data collection, and a plain-language privacy policy.
  • Accessible: 124 languages and local payment methods.
  • Evolving: improved continuously with your feedback.

Gink is more than an assistant: it's the bridge between you and your digital world.

Looking forward

Next on the way: Gink on iPhone through TestFlight, installers for Windows and Linux, the web version, and plugins so the community can teach Gink new skills.
