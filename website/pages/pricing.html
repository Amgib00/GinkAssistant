How paying works

Prepaid, never automatic

Choose 1, 3 or 12 months and pay once. Gink Pro never renews by itself; buying again adds time to the end of your current period.

Local ways to pay

Gink detects your country and shows the methods you already use. Where a local method isn't available yet, you can pay by card.

Questions

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free account keeps your chats in sync and protects your daily allowance. Sign in with email, Google, GitHub, Microsoft or Apple.

What happens when I reach the daily limit?

Gink tells you, and your allowance resets the next day. Natural voice switches to your device's own voice instead of going silent.

Something went wrong with a payment?

Write to gink@ginkaisupport.com with the date and the payment method, and we'll help. See the terms.
