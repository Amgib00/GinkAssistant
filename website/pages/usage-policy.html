Usage policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Gink is for learning, working and creating. This policy lists what it must never be used for — by anyone, in any Gink app, on the web, in Gink Genie, or through the Developer API, agents and SDKs. If you build on Gink, the people using what you built must follow it too.

Never use Gink to

  • Harm children. No sexual content involving anyone under 18, no grooming, and nothing that helps exploit or endanger a child. We report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.
  • Break the law, or help someone else do it: fraud, scams, money laundering, trafficking, buying or selling illegal goods.
  • Make weapons — biological, chemical, nuclear or radiological weapons, explosives or other weapons meant to cause mass harm — or plan violence or terrorism.
  • Attack computers or networks: malware, ransomware, breaking into accounts or systems you don’t own, or getting around security.
  • Deceive people: phishing pages, pages that imitate a real bank, government or company, fake reviews or documents, or impersonating a real person without their agreement.
  • Harass or hurt people: threats, bullying, hate against people for who they are, or encouraging self-harm.
  • Invade privacy: tracking or watching people without their knowledge, collecting personal data you have no right to, finding where a private person lives, or recognising strangers’ faces.
  • Make intimate images of real people without their consent, or sexual content that isn’t legal where you are.
  • Mislead about elections or public health: false information about how, when or where to vote, or campaigns built to deceive.
  • Spam or abuse the service: bulk unwanted messages, getting around limits, reselling access without our agreement, or overloading Gink.

Decisions about people need a person

Don’t let Gink decide alone about someone’s health, treatment, legal case, job, credit, insurance, housing, education or benefits. Gink can help prepare and explain, but a qualified person makes the decision and checks the facts. Gink is not a medical device: never use it to diagnose anyone, and don’t enter patient data in a workspace in healthcare mode.

Be open about AI

  • If people talk to something you built on Gink, make it clear they’re talking to an AI, and never claim to be human when someone sincerely asks.
  • Check AI-made content before you publish it, and label it where the law or the platform asks you to.

Agents, voice, camera and screen

  • Use agents, scheduled tasks and webhooks only on systems and data you’re allowed to use.
  • Record voices, point the camera or share a screen only where the people around you would agree, and never to secretly capture others.

Teenagers

Accounts of people under 18 get stricter safety everywhere, and parents can add controls. Don’t try to get around them.

What happens if this policy is broken

Gink may refuse a request, and we may limit or close accounts, revoke API keys or stop agents that break this policy. Serious cases, such as child exploitation or threats to life, may be reported to the authorities.

Report misuse

Seen Gink being misused, or got an answer that breaks this policy? Use the thumbs-down under the answer, or write to gink@ginkaisupport.com. Security problems: see the responsible disclosure policy.
