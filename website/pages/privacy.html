Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version: Gink keeps your account and the things you choose to sync or create, uses trusted services to answer you, never sells your data, and lets you download or delete everything from Settings.

Who we are

Gink is an AI assistant app for computers and phones, made by Gink AI (developer: Amgib00). This policy covers the Gink apps, the Gink server they connect to, and this website. Questions: gink@ginkaisupport.com.

“Hey Gink” and the microphone

“Hey Gink” is off until you switch it on. Listening for the wake word happens entirely on your device; no audio leaves it until Gink hears the wake word. Gink pauses that listening while it speaks, during dictation and during voice conversations.

Error reports

When something breaks in a Gink app, Gink sends what went wrong and where in Gink’s own code (the kind of error, its message, the app version and your platform) so we can fix it. Never your chats, files or what you typed; emails and keys are removed. Turn it off in Settings, then Privacy, then “Send error reports”. We keep only the most recent reports.

Helping improve Gink (off unless you turn it on)

Adults can choose to share their Gink Genie sessions to help train Gink’s own models: Settings, then Privacy, then “Help improve Gink”. What’s shared: your Genie requests, Genie’s steps and the code it writes — never your chats. Keys and passwords are removed automatically and private files (like .env) are never shared. You can stop at any time, or stop and delete what you shared. Teenagers can’t turn it on.

Workspaces

If you join a workspace (for example your hospital, school or company), its admins see your name and how many messages you sent each day — never your chats — and Gink follows the workspace’s instructions for you. In healthcare mode, Gink refuses messages that look like a patient’s details: workspaces must not be used with patient data. You join only after accepting an invitation, and you can leave at any time.

Parental controls

A parent can link their account to a teenager’s (13 to 17), only after the teenager accepts. The parent can set quiet hours and a daily limit and switch features off; they never see the teen’s chats. If Gink notices a serious risk (for example self-harm), it can tell the parent the kind of risk — never the words — and it never tells a parent about harm at home. The teen sees every notice sent about them.

What we never do

  • We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising.
  • We don't put AI service keys inside the app; they stay on our server.
  • We don't read your files. Gink only touches a file when you ask it to (for example, “find my CV”), and it doesn't upload it unless you attach it to a message.

How long we keep it

Your account and synced data stay until you delete them. Usage counts are kept per day. When you delete your account:

  • Your account, synced data and usage records are erased, together with your Gink Genie projects, websites, shared links, agent tasks and schedules, API keys and everything else that names your account.
  • Payment records are kept for our accounts, with your email replaced by an anonymous code.
  • Your email address and the date are kept in a list of deleted accounts, so that any device still signed in to the account is signed out.

See how to delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export your data: Settings, then Privacy, then Export (what’s on your device), and “Download everything on the server” (one ZIP with everything our server keeps about you; passwords and keys are never included).
  • Correct your profile: Settings, then Account.
  • Forget memories: Settings, then Memory.
  • Delete chats, or your whole account, in Settings.
  • Turn off “Hey Gink”, read-aloud and the briefing's “pick up where you left off” at any time.

You can also write to us at gink@ginkaisupport.com to make any of these requests.

Children

Gink is not meant for children under 13. We ask for a birth year and don’t open an account for anyone under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it. Teenagers (13 to 17) get stricter safety in every Gink feature.

Where your data is processed

Gink’s server and the services above may process your information in other countries, including the United States. We use them only to provide Gink, as described here.

Security

Connections to our server are encrypted, passwords are never stored in readable form, and access to the server is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a problem, please report it privately.

This website

This website uses no cookies, trackers, analytics or web fonts, and loads nothing from other companies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
