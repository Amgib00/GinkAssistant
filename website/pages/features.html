Features

Everything Gink can do today, grouped by what you're doing.

Talk

  • “Hey Gink” wakes Gink hands-free on your computer. The edges of the window glow while it listens, and it keeps listening for a follow-up.
  • Voice conversations with a full-screen view: Gink starts answering after the first sentence instead of making you wait.
  • 11 natural voices with their own personalities, including Nigerian, Kenyan, British and French voices.
  • Dictation to type with your voice in any chat.
  • 124 languages for Gink's answers, with native names to pick from.

Do

  • Alarms, timers, reminders and calendar events, set in a sentence.
  • Tasks with steps in your Workspace, with progress Gink keeps track of.
  • Messages, calls and email drafts opened ready to send.
  • Live information weather, air quality, sunrise, prayer times, currency, crypto, time zones and exact maths.
  • Videos and news found and shown as cards, plus Live TV channels.

On your computer

  • Open apps, folders and websites by name or nickname, including Finder and Terminal in any folder.
  • Music and volume play, pause and skip in Spotify or Apple Music.
  • Files find, open and show in Finder; see what you worked on recently.
  • Your Mac screenshots, dark mode, lock or sleep, Apple Notes, the clipboard, battery and storage.

Create

  • Files on request PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, Markdown and web pages, shown as cards you can open or save.
  • Images from a description, in the chat or in Media Studio with styles and shapes.
  • Code with highlighting, line numbers, copy and save.
  • Attachments photos (Pro), PDFs and documents for Gink to read.

Remember

  • Morning briefing with weather, plans and a headline, plus the tasks, chats and files you were working on, and a nudge to finish them.
  • Memory of the things you ask Gink to keep in mind.
  • Instructions that shape every answer, such as “keep it short”.
  • Your profile filled in from Google, GitHub, Microsoft or Apple sign-in.

Everywhere

  • Sync chats, tasks, memories and profile across your devices.
  • History that saves itself with smart titles, pins and drafts.
  • Light and dark themes, keyboard shortcuts and reduced motion.
  • Platforms macOS, Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone.
