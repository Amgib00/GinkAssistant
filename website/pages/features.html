Everything Gink can do

Grouped by what you’re doing. All of it works on your computer and your phone, and most of it in your browser too.

Talk

  • “Hey Gink” wakes Gink hands-free on your computer. The edges of the window glow while it listens, and it keeps listening for a follow-up.
  • Voice messages: hold the microphone, talk, let go. Gink writes what you said, in any language, and answers out loud.
  • Voice conversations with a full-screen view: Gink starts answering after your first sentence instead of making you wait.
  • 11 natural voices with their own personalities, including Nigerian, Kenyan, British and French voices.
  • 124 languages for Gink’s answers, with native names to pick from, and dictation in any chat.

Show

  • Screen view: say “look at my screen” and Gink sees what you see, only while you share it. On a Mac, Gink asks once for Screen Recording permission (Gink Pro).
  • Live camera: point your camera and ask. A picture is sent only when you ask, and the camera switches off when you leave (Gink Pro).
  • Photos and documents: attach photos (Gink Pro), PDFs and documents for Gink to read.

Do

  • An agent that finishes the job: for a task with several steps, Gink writes a plan you can watch, works through it and keeps going by itself until every step is done.
  • Alarms, timers, reminders and calendar events, set in a sentence.
  • Tasks with steps in your Workspace, with progress Gink keeps track of.
  • Messages, calls and email drafts opened ready to send.
  • Live information: weather, air quality, sunrise, prayer times, currency, crypto, time zones and exact maths.

On your computer

  • Your apps: find any installed app, open it, see what’s running and close what you don’t need. An app with unsaved work still asks you first.
  • Your code editor: “open my portfolio in VS Code” opens the project in VS Code, Cursor, Zed, Xcode, PyCharm, Sublime Text and others.
  • Text and code files: Gink reads, writes and edits them in your home folder and keeps a backup of every file it changes. Hidden and system files stay off-limits.
  • Folders and websites by name or nickname, including Finder and Terminal in any folder.
  • Music, volume and your Mac: Spotify and Apple Music, screenshots, dark mode, Apple Notes, battery and storage.

Create

  • Maps: when an answer is about places, a real street map appears in the chat by itself, light or dark like the app: each place marked and named, with a photo, what kind of place it is and directions in your own maps app. Zoom in to the buildings, or open it bigger.
  • Files on request: PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, Markdown and web pages, shown as cards you can open or save.
  • Images from a description, in the chat or in Media Studio with styles and shapes.
  • Apps and websites with Gink Genie: describe one and Genie builds the whole project with you, then shares a preview when you choose.
  • Code with highlighting, line numbers, copy and save.

Remember

  • Memory from your chats: lasting things you mention, like where you live or your work, are remembered and shown with Undo.
  • Sensitive topics stay out of memory unless you turn on “Include sensitive topics in memory”.
  • Import memory from other AI providers: copy one prompt into your old assistant, paste its answer into Gink.
  • Morning briefing with weather, plans and a headline, plus the tasks, chats and files you were working on.
  • Instructions that shape every answer, such as “keep it short”.

Everywhere

  • Sync for chats, tasks, memories and your profile, across your devices.
  • Location metadata: answers fit where you are, from your device’s time zone and language. Turn it off to send only your clock’s difference from UTC.
  • Privacy choices: every policy one tap away in Settings, and your data ready to download or delete.
  • Platforms: Mac, Windows, Linux, Android and the web. iPhone is on its way through TestFlight.

Build

  • The Developer API: chat, search, maps and places, voice, memory and many requests at once, for your own app.
  • Managed agents: up to 60 steps in a workspace that keeps its files, on a schedule if you like, with webhooks when they finish.
  • Python and JavaScript SDKs and a CLI: pip install gink-ai or npm install gink-ai. Start building.
