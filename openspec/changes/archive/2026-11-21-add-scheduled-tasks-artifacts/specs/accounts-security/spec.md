## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sign-in that shows what Gink does
The sign-in page on the web and in the apps SHALL keep every way to sign in and, where there is room, show what Gink does now and what Gink Pro adds, with a link to the plans that opens outside the app; on phones the form SHALL come first.

#### Scenario: A wide window
- **WHEN** the web sign-in opens on a computer
- **THEN** the form is beside a panel showing a scheduled task and its result, and Gink Pro with See plans

#### Scenario: A phone
- **WHEN** the sign-in opens on a phone
- **THEN** only the form shows, with Gink's name above it and a short Gink Pro line below
