## Why

The owner asked for "Hey Gink" and just "Gink" to wake Gink the same way everywhere, Media Studio
without video and with a gallery of ready-made looks, and only Gink's own models in the pickers (the
Settings list showed the provider's raw and retired models).

## What Changes

- Wake on "Hey Gink" or, if allowed (Settings > Voice > Also wake on just "Gink", on by default), on
  "Gink" at the start of what was heard -- computers (Vosk "ginkgo"), Android, web (with the same glow).
- Media Studio: no Video tab; an Ideas row of 11 looks (Zen, Claw machine, Chibi keychain, Elven,
  Sitcom, 8-bit game, Football figurine, Landscape, Statue, Underwater, Cozy room) that fill in an
  editable prompt with a style and shape; card art drawn with gradients and symbols.
- Model lists in Settings, the composer and the web app: Gink's models only; an old non-Gink choice
  moves to Gink Crystal.

## Impact

- Modified spec: languages-voice. Apps, web app.
